Published researchFinal-year project · 2025

IntelliBin

A hospital waste sorter that sees each item, classifies it on a Raspberry Pi and drops it into the right bin.

Problem
Hospital waste is often sorted by hand, and mixing categories creates infection and disposal risks for staff.
What I did
As first author, I assembled the dataset, fine-tuned MobileNetV2 into five hospital waste categories, deployed it on the Pi and wired the camera, ultrasonic sensor, stepper and servo motors into one cycle.
Recognition
Overall Best Project and Best Department Project, Biomedical Engineering, 2025.
Stack
Python, TensorFlow / TensorFlow Lite, MobileNetV2, OpenCV, Raspberry Pi 4B (4 GB), OV5647 camera
Read the paper ↗
97%Accuracy in a 100-item real-time test
8–12 sFrom detection to deposit, per item
6,868Training images, 1,475 captured by our team
5Hospital waste categories

How one item moves through it

  1. DetectUltrasonic sensor notices an item
  2. CaptureOV5647 camera takes a frame
  3. ClassifyMobileNetV2 runs on the Pi
  4. SortStepper and servo route it

Prototype results from controlled testing. Pharmaceutical waste was the weakest class at 85%. Not clinically validated.
