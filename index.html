Published researchFinal-year project, 2025

IntelliBin

A hospital waste sorter that sees each item, classifies it on a Raspberry Pi and drops it into the right bin.

97%accuracy in a 100-item real-time test
8–12 sfrom detection to deposit
6,868training images, 1,475 captured by our team
  1. DetectAn ultrasonic sensor notices an item.
  2. CaptureThe camera takes a frame.
  3. ClassifyMobileNetV2 picks one of five waste types on the Pi.
  4. SortStepper and servo motors route it to its bin.
One cycle per item, start to finish.Overall Best Project and Best Department Project, 2025

The problem

Hospital waste is often sorted by hand, and mixing categories creates infection and disposal risks for staff.

What I did

As first author, I assembled the dataset, fine-tuned MobileNetV2, deployed it on the Pi and wired the camera, sensor and motors into one cycle.

Read the paper

Built with

Python, TensorFlow and TensorFlow Lite, MobileNetV2, OpenCV, Raspberry Pi 4B and an OV5647 camera.

Prototype results from controlled testing. Pharmaceutical waste was the weakest class at 85%. Not clinically validated.
